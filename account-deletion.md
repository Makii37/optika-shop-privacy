# Account Deletion – Jovović Shop

**App:** Jovović Shop (Optika Jovović)  
**Developer:** Jovović Shop / gsx.studi@gmail.com

## How to delete your account in the app

1. Open the **Jovović Shop** app and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Open **Account security** (Sigurnost naloga).
4. Tap **Delete profile** (Obriši profil).
5. Confirm deletion.

Your account and associated profile data will be deleted from our systems.

## Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email **gsx.studi@gmail.com** with subject **Account deletion request** and include the email address linked to your account.

## Data deleted

- Account profile (name, email, phone, address, profile photo)
- Authentication data (Firebase Auth account)
- Favorites and cart data linked to your account
- Push notification token (FCM)

## Data that may be retained

- **Order records** may be kept as required by law (tax, accounting) for a limited period, then anonymized or deleted according to our retention policy.
- **Price-on-request offer records** may be kept up to **30 days** for security and dispute resolution, then deleted or anonymized.
- **Technical logs** (e.g. crash/analytics) may be retained in aggregated or anonymized form as described in our Privacy Policy.
