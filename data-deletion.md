# Data Deletion Request – Jovović Shop

**App:** Jovović Shop (Optika Jovović)

## Request deletion of some or all data (without deleting your account)

Email **gsx.studi@gmail.com** with subject **Data deletion request** and include:
- The email address linked to your account
- What data you want deleted (e.g. profile photo, address, favorites, chat messages)

We will process your request in line with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

## Delete data in the app (without deleting your account)

- **Profile → Edit profile** – update or remove personal details
- **Favorites** – remove saved products
- **Cart** – clear cart items
